Tribes Rivals: Clan Wars (community mod)

Extract the entire ZIP into a permanent folder before launching.
Both launchers use the bundled maps in "Client Files". Keep the folder beside them.

WINDOWS
Double-click "Start Clan Wars.bat".

LINUX / STEAM PROTON
Open a terminal in the extracted folder and run:
    sh "Clan Wars - Linux.sh" --setup
In Steam, open TRIBES 3: Rivals > Properties.
Enable Proton under Compatibility, and paste the printed line into General > Launch Options.
Then click Play in Steam.

Both versions require Steam and an installed copy of TRIBES 3: Rivals.
The client checks GameServer01 for signed updates when it opens.
